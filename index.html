ABOUT MALIKOV

MORE THAN COCKTAILS.
IT'S A CRAFT.

Every territory has a story to tell through taste. I aim to showcase Tunisia through its ingredients, traditions and distinctive terroir, transforming local products into contemporary cocktail experiences.

Malik Khouaja — Malikov — is a luxury mixologist and beverage creator based in Tunis. A beverage professional with four years behind the bar, he combines refined service, cocktail creativity and strong guest engagement in luxury hospitality and events. Beyond the glass, the work is to help build a contemporary identity for Tunisian mixology: exploring new flavour combinations, documenting original creations, and carrying that expression internationally through responsible technique, homemade ingredients and a more conscious use of resources.
